About Ify Rose Craft Work

Every piece starts with a measurement, not a guess.

Ify Rose Craft Work makes school bags, office bags, laptop bags, Bible covers and padded instrument cases for violin, guitar and piano by hand — and repairs any bag that's earned a few battle scars.

Nothing leaves the workbench until the seams are straight and the fit is exact — that's the standard whether it's a child's first school bag or a guitar case built to protect an instrument on every trip.
